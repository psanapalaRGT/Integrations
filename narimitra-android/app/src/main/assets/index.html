What we hold about you

Your phone number, your building, and the tips you chose to add. No contacts, no location history, no call recordings. Under the DPDP Act you can take all of it out or delete it, and the button below does exactly that without a support ticket.

NariMitra does not run police verification. When a listing says a document was seen, it means a named neighbour saw it, not that we checked it. Verify before you hire someone into your home.
